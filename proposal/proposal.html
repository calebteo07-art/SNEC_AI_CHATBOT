01Executive Summary
Every ophthalmic allied-health professional deserves a tutor who never sleeps, a patient who is always available, and a curriculum that adapts to exactly what they keep forgetting.

SNEC EyeBot is an AI-powered learning companion built on SNEC’s own OAOT Module 1 curriculum. It unites three evidence-based modalities — a Socratic AI tutor, interactive clinical case simulations, and spaced-repetition flashcards — into one continuous loop that personalises itself to each learner. Running quietly alongside is an analytics layer that gives educators, for the first time, a live view of where an entire cohort is thriving or struggling. The platform is fully engineered, demonstrable today, and ready for a SNEC pilot.

02The Challenge

Training the eyes
behind the eye care.

Ophthalmic Assistants, Technicians and Patient Service Associates are the first clinical touchpoint at SNEC. Their training is rigorous — and rightly so — but it meets four structural limits that no amount of dedication can fully solve.

Expert time is scarce

One educator cannot give every trainee unlimited one-to-one tutoring or on-demand case practice.

Case exposure is uneven

Which emergencies a trainee actually meets is left to the luck of the clinical roster.

Knowledge decays

Without deliberate reinforcement, much of what is taught is forgotten within weeks of the lesson.

Cohort gaps are invisible

Educators often discover weaknesses at assessment time — when it is already late to intervene.

EyeBot is engineered to dissolve all four — at once.

03The Solution

One companion. Three modes.
A single adaptive loop.

Mode 01

Socratic Tutor

Plain-language questions met with Explanation → Mechanism → Clinical Pearl → a check for understanding, always grounded in the OAOT knowledge base.

Mode 02

Case Simulations

Interview a virtual patient, order investigations, commit to a diagnosis and plan — graded on a four-domain rubric out of 40 with instant feedback.

Mode 03

Spaced Flashcards

High-yield cards auto-generated from each session and every missed clinical point, scheduled by the SM-2 algorithm for durable recall.

…and quietly, an analytics layer watches the whole cohort — turning individual practice into institutional insight.
