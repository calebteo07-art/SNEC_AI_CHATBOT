## OSCE coverage (per pool)

OSCE = practical/scenario stations, resolved by pool (OA/PSA → CLINICAL, OT → OT).
Procedural competencies each keep a station; knowledge domains are exercised
*within* case scenarios (disease recognition, triage/escalation,
pharmacology-in-context, ethics/communication) and mapped here — never silently
skipped. Full OA/PSA → shared-CLINICAL merge is the separate OSCE plan.
