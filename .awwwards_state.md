**Art direction: DARK ADAPTATION** — the UI is a darkened ophthalmic examination theatre. Colors are instrument lights (never paint); transitions are optical phenomena: blink wipes (routes), focus-pull/accommodation (entrances), dilation (radial), saccades (30–60ms staggers).
